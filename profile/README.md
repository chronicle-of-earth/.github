<p align="center"><strong>History, in space and time.</strong></p>

Chronicle of Earth is an evidence-linked historical atlas in development.
Choose a place and a date, see what changed, and inspect the evidence behind
every assertion: its sources, its temporal bounds, how far it has been
verified, and who disagrees.

### Explore

- **[Website preview](https://chronicle-of-earth.github.io/)**: the record
  on a turning globe, with evidence bars, verification stages and coverage gaps
- **[Method](https://chronicle-of-earth.github.io/method/)**: how entities,
  claims, evidence, time and uncertainty are modelled
- **[Roadmap](https://chronicle-of-earth.github.io/roadmap/)**: milestones
  M0–M4 and their exit criteria
- **[Brand](https://chronicle-of-earth.github.io/brand/)**: the chronicle
  globe, the brandtype and the usage rules

The preview is unindexed and may change without notice.

### Status

Early. The data foundation and a local globe experiment exist. The historical
records are a small set of machine-assisted **drafts**; none has been reviewed
by a person yet, and no historical borders have been reconstructed. The
editorial scope is 1900–2026.

### Commitments

- Evidence before assertion: every record carries sources, a temporal scope
  and a confidence rationale.
- Control, claims and recognition kept separate; disagreement preserved, not
  resolved out of sight.
- Missing is not absent: coverage gaps are published as gaps.
- Present-day reference maps are always labelled as such, never passed off as
  history.
- No accounts, analytics or advertising on the website.
